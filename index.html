Menulytr · Layout study

One restaurant.
Five ways to read the menu.

Osteria Lume, an invented pizzeria in Trastevere, rendered five ways. Same dishes, same photos, same palette. Pick a layout below, then tune the colors and type from the panel in the top right. Your palette follows you between layouts.

01Carta

A printed carta, single column with dotted leaders. Hover a dish on desktop and its photo floats beside the cursor.

Open Carta

02Mosaico

Photo-first grid with a sliding filter pill and a detail view for every dish. A soft three.js form breathes in the header.

Open Mosaico

03Bottega

Sticky sidebar with scroll-spy on desktop, chip navigation on phones. Compact cards with thumbnails.

Open Bottega

04Tavola

Five full-height chapters. The photo panel stays pinned and swaps as you hover or scroll through the dishes.

Open Tavola

05Lavagna

A dense board in columns with instant search and highlighted matches. Press slash to search. Sections fold on phones.

Open Lavagna
